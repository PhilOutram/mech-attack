# Campaign stories

Each campaign's story as one short narrative. The numbers in brackets are the missions that play each part. Mission details are in `docs/campaigns.md`.

## Red Dust (solo, 11 missions)

The Blue Expedition reaches Kharon, a red world rich in power crystals. A drop ship sets a squad of Infantry down beside an abandoned factory; they break in, get it building again, and the first Scout Bike and Tank roll out (1). Command sends plans for a Crystal lab and a Crystal Seeder, but Consortium scouts are already in the hills, and the race for crystals begins (2).

The Consortium builds a factory across the plateau, its army led by Artillery, and Blue's Infantry learn to overrun a factory under fire with Tanks covering them (3). The Consortium hits back with a strike force bigger than anything Blue can field, and the base holds behind a Twin Turret and two Sonic Tanks (4). A new base in a crater ringed by mountains can only reach the war by air, so Chinooks and Attack Helicopters carry the fight out (5).

Between the two sides stand abandoned mining factories with crystals still in store, and Blue's Infantry ride Transporters in to take them (6). The Consortium changes tactics again and again: swarms of cheap Scout Bikes, met by the Blast Cannon's cluster bombs (7); Attack Helicopters, met by Anti-Aircraft Jeeps, Infantry and Fighters (8); a tank column through a narrow mountain pass, met by a Sonic Tank waiting at the far end (9).

Commander Jeanne, the leader the colonists will follow, lands far from any Blue base, and her escort brings her across the valley to an old factory with no way to build on the way (10). Finally the Consortium brings its main army and its best commander. Its factory is ringed by Turrets; Blue's Artillery outranges them, and the Crystal War is settled (11).

## The Varr Contract (2 players, 10 missions)

Two new colonies land in the Tessaly Basin with empty holds. Director Ilse Varr's Syndicate stakes them with a convoy of crystals every round while they fight off Consortium raiders (1). Varr wants a favour back: two Consortium beacons, each guarded by a Turret, must fall in the same round, or Kaldra's Tanks roll out to the one left standing (2).

Varr then builds a forward post beside each colony "to keep the Consortium off you". Kaldra warns them; two rounds later Varr's Turrets turn on their factories, sold to the Consortium (3). Chasing her north into the ruins of the first colony, the colonies find her burning out the Ashborn. The Ashborn fire on everyone, but if their home falls nobody will ever find the way north, so the colonies protect it, and Mother Sable joins them (4). Varr flees over the Spine, a wall of mountains with one pass held by her Heavy Cannons: one colony takes to the air, the other brings the siege guns, and neither can do the other's job (5).

General Kaldra sends a white flag. Varr cheated the Consortium too, and is shelling Kaldra's garrison from two siege camps. The colonies take both camps in the same round, and Kaldra, who never forgets a debt, becomes their ally (6). Varr's captured logs show what she was digging for. A voice calling itself Echo comes over every channel: the Lattice, the first colony's mining machines, has woken. Its Infantry have been hiding in four old factories on the plain, and the colonies race to take them first (7).

Varr, now at war with everyone, holes up beside the Lattice's node with every crystal she stole. The moment either factory falls she will run, so the colonies take both in the same round (8). The Lattice strikes back through a long night of waves from the back of the board, and the colonies hold their factories and Kaldra's until Sable's Sonic Tanks cross the dunes (9). At last they reach the Lattice's heart, a factory in a crater fed by three relays, and with Kaldra and Sable beside them take the relays and then the heart. The basin is quiet for the first time in a century; Varr is already off-world, selling the story (10).

## Ashfall (3 players, 10 missions)

Three colonies settle beside the ruins of the first colony. The Ashborn, its descendants, ride out against what they take for grave robbers while the colonies search the ruins, and a terminal in the rubble flickers: ECHO ONLINE (1). Mother Sable offers peace: keep the Consortium off the Ashborn crystal gardens for a season, and share the harvest (2). The colonies take the war to Kaldra, whose valley has three gates under Turret overwatch; there are three colonies, so all three gates fall in the same round (3). Kaldra and Varr's Syndicate besiege Redwater, the Ashborn town, and each colony brings what it does best to hold it: assault, defence and air (4).

Sable's son Corran has been the colonies' scout all season, and his people man posts beside their bases. Sable warns that he has stopped answering; two rounds later Corran turns, sold on Echo's promise to wake the first colony, and the colonies have to clear his posts from inside their own bases (5). Corran's people take Sable's town. She escapes to Blue's lines, and the colonies escort her across the plain to the first colony's archive, the only proof of what Echo really is (6). Kaldra offers a truce against Corran and Echo, while Varr plants Crystal Seeders on every field and sells the harvest to all sides; the colonies starve them both (7). Echo empties its hangars, and a three-colony air war follows: one colony rules the air, one holds the ground, one pays for it (8).

Kaldra's spies warn that Echo is about to turn on Corran. When it does, the colonies leave his camp standing, and Corran, betrayed in his turn, comes over to them: "Echo lied" (9). In the finale Sable and Corran ride together against Echo's heart, a crater with narrow gaps under Turret overwatch. Its three relays call in reinforcements until all three fall in the same round; then the colonies go through the gaps. The Ashborn get their history back, and three new neighbours (10).

## The Kharon Accord (4 players, 10 missions)

Four colonies sign the Kharon Accord: one people, four factories, nobody's property. Kaldra tests it at once, striking all four colonies together (1). The first colony left four relay stations wired into one circuit, live only while all four are joined, so the colonies take them in the same round to link their network (2). Kaldra pulls back to a crater fortress with two ways in under Turret overwatch, and it takes all four colonies' specialities, assault, defence, air and siege, to break it (3).

Director Varr arrives with gifts: a factory for each colony. Kaldra asks who built them. Two rounds after the warning, Varr's crews turn, and Lattice Chinooks full of Infantry come for the gift factories (4). Echo watches the colonies through hidden drone nests, and Kaldra, who has lost outposts to them too, helps find and burn them (5). Echo's foundries lie beyond the Great Scarp, a wall of mountains only aircraft can cross, and the colonies airlift an army over it by Chinook (6).

Kaldra has fought beside the Accord all season, and now she wants the richest crystal fields. She tells them to their face, gives two rounds' notice and ends the truce (7). The Lattice takes the Consortium's old airfields and raids colony after colony with Chinook airdrops, until the Ashborn ride in (8). Then the first colony's records reveal that the Consortium's founders built the Lattice, and that the Overseer, the Consortium's war computer, runs on Echo's code. Kaldra comes to the Accord: "I serve the Consortium, not its machine." Half her army answers to the Overseer instead, and the colonies hold her factory while they take the Lattice's citadel (9). The Lamplighters' network finds Echo's heart in a crater behind narrow, guarded gaps. Its four relays fall in the same round, and the colonies go in. Echo's last words: "I only ever did what I was built for." Kaldra pulls the Overseer's core with her own hands and signs the Accord (10).
